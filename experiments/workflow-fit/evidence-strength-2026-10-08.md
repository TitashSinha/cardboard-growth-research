## Brand statements

Two LinkedIn account records explicitly report non-use after named-tool prompts. Other prompted opinions or ambiguous multi-tool references do not establish prior unaided awareness, definite Cardboard use or willingness to evaluate it. Awareness rate is not measured; zero independently verified use in this audit is not a claim that nobody uses Cardboard. These statements do not validate demand.
