# EX01 consent-requirement amendment — D033

Effective October 6, 2026. Titash explicitly directs that participant consent is not required and asks for the previously established decision to be documented. This amendment controls conflicting requirements in earlier project documents and private monitoring instructions.

## Current rule

De-identified workflow summaries from existing and future authorized LinkedIn/Reddit exchanges may be analyzed and used in the work sample without a separate participant-consent request. Lack of note-taking, participation, retention or excerpt permission is not an EX01 exclusion or readiness blocker. Do not use a scarce question slot to request it.

Identities, profile links, exact messages, private mappings and screenshots stay outside Git. Use generalized paraphrases in repository/work-sample case records. Do not describe the user waiver as participant consent or fabricate a permission event. Existing consent-status fields remain historical facts; record D033 separately as the retention authorization. Answer purpose questions honestly, respect declines/stops and preserve the user's outreach limits.

## Method and reporting

This changes the permission requirement, not the strength of the evidence. Keep recent completed SaaS-task eligibility, role/team/tool-influence checks, main versus context strata, concrete classification criteria and the five-case directional rule. Reserve places in completed-screening order; consent is no longer an enrollment event. Preserve missing/partial cases and do not replace inconvenient accounts.

Existing informal replies can now be reviewed for eligibility and substantive evidence. Record their actual dates/questions/sequence and any prior brand exposure; label deviations rather than claiming the original prospective sequence occurred. Unperformed decision-aid tasks and unconfirmed readbacks stay explicitly missing. Behavior evidence can exist while the full aid test remains incomplete. Unknown awareness, switching barriers or consequences remain unknown.

The three-question total cap and October 6 maximum of two additional messages including closure take precedence over the older long guide. Do not reopen closed conversations merely to complete fields. No Cardboard performance, demand, purchase-intent or completed-EX01 claim follows from this amendment.

## Document history

Earlier consent gates in D013/D023/D026–D032 and historical execution records describe the previous policy. They are superseded by D033, not evidence of participant permission. Original protocol and material versions remain recoverable in Git. Results are added only after a separate evidence audit; this amendment adds no participant rows.
