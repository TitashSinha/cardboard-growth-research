## What the replies support

| Question | What respondents actually described, generalized | Decision supported |
|---|---|---|
| How is the work done? | Planning/storyboards, UI or graphic preparation, animation, voice/sound and final polish; tools and steps varied across accounts | Diagnose the stage before comparing editors. A broad request for faster video production hides different jobs |
| What is difficult? | Repeated versions/corrections, manual keyframes, easing/transitions and finishing; design/reference ideas did not always fully solve the job | Examine specific corrections and retained creative control. Time spent can include valued craft as well as unwanted rework |
| What matters when considering another workflow? | Nuance, timing, brand control, editable output, actual-interface fidelity, device fit and agent setup; generation could still need manual finishing | Include editability, corrections and whole-project/setup work in evaluation. Do not assume a familiar working editor needs replacing |
| What do they say about agentic tools/Cardboard? | Some explicitly reported not using the named platforms; other accounts gave general opinions about assistants or described AI generation plus manual editing | Preserve explicit non-use and prompted opinions. Ambiguous references to multiple named tools do not establish Cardboard use or interest in evaluating it |

These are self-reported observations, not hands-on product results. General advice, service offers, builder promotion and requests for context provide weaker workflow evidence than a concrete production account. Keep them separate rather than counting every reply as equally informative.

## Enough for which decision?

**Enough to close this learning round and make the editorial decisions:** separate production stages; distinguish generation from an editable project; require correction/control evidence; retain the option of keeping the current editor. Those decisions are embodied in article 1's documented revision, the five article jobs and the evaluation-page copy.

**Still unanswered where replies do not establish them:** why a particular person would actually switch; whether a problem is consequential and remains unresolved; prior unaided Cardboard awareness; definite Cardboard use where wording is ambiguous; interest in evaluating it; actual product performance and purchase behavior. Record each gap as unknown. Do not require another worksheet or reopen conversations to justify using the evidence already collected.
