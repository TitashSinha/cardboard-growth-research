- **Workflow observations:** respondents described planning/storyboards, interface or graphic preparation, animation, voice/sound and final polish. AI assistance served different jobs, including scripting, assets and rough assembly; generating clips and completing an editable product video were distinct tasks.

Human control over timing, storytelling and brand nuance remained important in several accounts. Time-consuming work alone does not establish a material unresolved switching problem.
- **Context on alternatives:** one account contrasted costly noneditable generated output with a cheaper actual-interface workflow. Other accounts described platform dependency or agent setup effort. These are self-reports and context, not measured comparative performance or proof that Cardboard solves the problem.
- **Awareness limits:** Titash manually named ElevenLabs and Cardboard in later questions. Resulting opinions are prompted. Explicit non-use can be recorded where stated; ambiguous references to AI tools cannot establish Cardboard use. Prior unaided awareness, actual Cardboard use and willingness to evaluate remain separate, with missing values unknown.

These observations support a cautious editorial direction: explain generation versus editable production, and show what control and corrections a workflow preserves. They do not support promised savings, purchase recommendations, broad switching demand or Cardboard product-fit claims.
