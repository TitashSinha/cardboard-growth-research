# Collection method

Date: 2026-10-02, Asia/Kolkata. Independent convenience sample, not a market census.

## Google (E026)

Twenty deliberately chosen queries cover category, workflow, buyer segment, comparison, price, platform, and objections. Chrome desktop, existing signed-in account; URL requests `hl=en&gl=us&pws=0`. Google footer displayed results not personalized and location unknown. **US English is requested, not a verified US-location rank test.**

Read live rendered pages through the supported browser UI. Count ordinary linked result headings in order, excluding AIO text, videos/discussion modules without ordinary result headings, sitelinks and People also ask. No sponsored label appeared in the later collected pages; Q01–Q05 were reviewed from their visible snapshots. Counts vary by page. These are positions among the inspected ordinary results, not a universal top-ten ranking. No pagination or automated Google HTTP scraping used. Record the first ordinary result and all observed official Cardboard positions in `search-observations.json`; CSV is derived. Original domains, including usecardboard.com, are preserved. We did not endorse result-page claims.

No official Cardboard result appeared in the inspected ordinary headings for the 16 nonbrand queries. Two branded queries and one site diagnostic surfaced official URLs. This does not prove absence elsewhere on the page, in other locations, deeper results, or the Google index. Q08 is branded comparison intent and is excluded from nonbrand counts. No search-volume data, traffic forecasts, or rank estimates obtained.

## ChatGPT (E025)

Existing account, Chat mode, fresh temporary unpersonalized conversation per prompt. Search explicitly requested in the prompt; source links must appear before marking search observed. Model picker showed GPT-5.6 Sol. The first run used Medium effort; new chats defaulted to Instant thereafter, recorded separately. This difference prevents treating all tests as a controlled identical-model-setting experiment. Record only Cardboard product mentions, recommendations and official-domain citations, not generic uses of the word cardboard. Branded prompts measure retrieval accuracy, not unaided discovery. Repeated prompt tests are separate observations, not independent people.

Results are summarized as observed brand ordering and visible citation URLs. No private account/sidebar content is saved. Temporary conversations may expire; reproduce with the exact prompt and settings in the CSV. Answers themselves are not capability evidence: follow primary sources before reusing factual claims.

## Public website (E008–E010)

`python analysis/collect_public.py` captures HTTP status, final URL, canonical, robots, titles, links and body hashes with four independent requests at a time. It reads only public pages and stores metadata, not full copyrighted pages. No production modification. UTC capture timestamps are retained in JSON; project dates use Asia/Kolkata. Sitemap counts describe listed URLs, not indexed pages. Browser readback resolves interactive pricing and FAQ states.

## Buyer evidence

Purposive public discussions include Cardboard's historical HN launch and adjacent SaaS workflows. Contributors are not verified customers; promotional bias and survivorship bias are possible. One statement does not establish prevalence or willingness to pay. Quotes are short and linked; interpretations are labeled hypotheses.
