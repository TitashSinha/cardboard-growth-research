# Product and offer audit

Public documentation and live-browser review, 2026-10-02. No firsthand editing test. Evidence IDs resolve in [sources.csv](sources.csv).

## What a buyer can verify

Cardboard's homepage presents creation from prompts and existing footage, chat revisions, an editable timeline, captions, reframing, sharing and export. It also promotes generative media. Examples and a first-use explanation already exist (E001). These are vendor demonstrations, not independently reproduced outcomes. Do not position Cardboard as only a generator or claim that it alone offers conversational editing.

The Mac page distinguishes local processing and Apple Silicon access from the browser route. It advertises complimentary Lite with three 1080p exports and no card needed; connecting an external agent requires an eligible existing subscription (E006). This project's Windows host cannot validate that Mac experience. Free Mac access should not become a universal free-trial CTA.

## Proof and first use

The stronger hypothesis is **make existing proof easier to evaluate for one buyer job**. A historical HN participant wanted raw assets alongside outputs; the founder replied that sample projects and prompts existed (E011). That is counterevidence to a claim that proof never existed. The old demo URL now redirects to the homepage (E017). We did not establish whether equivalent samples remain available after signup.

Proposed test: one real SaaS recording, exact prompt, unedited input, finished output, manual corrections and export inspection shown together. Compare that package against the current example-led explanation. Do not invent an output or claim a time saving.
