## Screen eligibility under D033

## Neutral questions — about 15–20 minutes

1. Tell me about the last product video you finished. What was it for, when was it due, where did it go and what did you deliver?
2. Walk me through what you actually did from source material to delivery. Which tools and people were involved?
3. What worked well enough that you would keep it? What was already familiar or paid for?
4. Tell me about a correction you actually made. What was wrong, how did you notice it and how did you fix it? If none, record that.
5. What consequence did that correction have? Ask for specifics only after their account. If they estimate time, label it estimated; no invented precision.
6. Who approved the video? How did feedback arrive, and what did you hand off? What had to remain editable?
7. What did you spend on that project or existing tools, including zero? Separate recurring subscription, freelancer and already-owned tools; declining to answer is valid.
8. Did you consider or try another method for that project? What did you actually try, keep, replace or abandon—and why?
9. Has that same task/problem occurred before? Ask for another actual example; no assumption of weekly frequency.
10. What remains unresolved today? What would count as an acceptable result for this specific task?

Do not lead with “Would you use Cardboard?” or hypothetical payment questions. Do not equate time spent with wasted time. Probe contradictions politely: “You said the result met the deadline, but approval was difficult. How did those fit together?”

## Decision-aid task and closing

After the behavior questions, ask prior Cardboard familiarity and show [v1.1](../experiments/workflow-fit/materials/decision-aid-v1.1.md). Ask the participant to map their actual project, choose the route and point out unclear/missing parts. Ask what, if anything, the sheet gets wrong. Do not steer toward switching or supply a fictional Cardboard result.

Read back factual notes only within current conversation budgets; record unconfirmed notes honestly. Use de-identified paraphrases under D033, with no participant-consent request. Exact quotes remain private. Thank them; no tool signup or follow-up message without authorization.
