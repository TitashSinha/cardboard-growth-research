# Competitor evidence and implications

Independent public-source track, 3 October 2026. No accounts, purchases or firsthand product use. [Comparison](competitors.csv) separates documented offers from inferred segment fit. Prices are page observations, not transaction quotes; currency is dollars as displayed unless stated explicitly. Do not rank products by untested quality.

## What challenges the proposed position

**Conversational editing is not a defensible exclusive claim.** Descript and Kapwing document it (C001, C006). Kapwing is especially direct counterevidence to exclusivity around browser access plus chat plus editable timeline. A comparison must assess the specific buyer task and the correction burden, not check whether each tool has an AI label.

**Real product footage is a crowded job.** Screen Studio imports existing recordings (C003); Clueso explicitly addresses product workflows and launches (C008). Their existence supports the relevance of the task but does not establish its market size, dissatisfaction or Cardboard's advantage. Screen Studio's Mac-only scope creates an eligibility distinction for Windows prospects, not a general quality advantage for Cardboard.

**Integration is also not exclusive.** Clueso advertises external AI-client connections (C008); Descript lists timeline export (C001). Do not imply competitors lock every project inside their own environment. Export compatibility needs a practical test with the buyer's destination software.

**The strongest substitute may cost no new software money.** Existing-suite skill, a plain walkthrough, or text/screenshots can be sufficient. The project has no evidence that polished video improves buyer conversion. Outsourcing also delegates judgment, so comparing subscription price with a freelancer's total fee would omit valuable work.

## Segment comparison: hypotheses to validate

| Segment | Plausible recurring job | Competitive hurdle | Evidence missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small SaaS marketing team | Release walkthrough, launch edit, follow-up clip | Screen Studio/Clueso specialization; existing recording habit | Actual frequency, acceptable correction time, willingness to pay |
| Agency | Multiple client versions and approval cycles | Collaboration, permissions and editable handoff can dominate initial-cut speed | Cardboard's current client-review controls and delivery compatibility |
| Professional creator | Long recordings, repeat episodes, short clips | Existing editing skill; Descript/Opus specialization | Long-project reliability, style consistency, recurring economics |

These are qualitative fit considerations, not scored research findings. They favor testing a narrow SaaS launch workflow first because its fidelity requirement can be demonstrated clearly. They do not establish that this audience is larger or more profitable.

## Recommended proof design — proposal

Compare Cardboard with the participant's current method on the same consented product recording. Have the participant define an acceptable result before testing. Record setup, editing, corrections, unusable output, export inspection and whether they would use it for the next release. Preserve failures. A claim of better speed or fidelity requires actual observations; a polished vendor example alone cannot supply it.
