## What this plan helps decide

First determine whether a recent buyer's current workflow leaves a material unresolved job (H03/EX01). Then ask whether offer clarity (O01), evaluable existing proof (O02) or a task-specific decision route (O03) addresses that job. A factual price discrepancy warrants correction but does not establish switching demand. A satisfactory generator/editor combination is a legitimate alternative, not automatically a problem to solve.

Use three separate evidence streams: public replies; eligible, consented recent-project accounts and prototype tasks; future company events. Never join Reddit accounts to participant IDs or pool these streams into a conversion rate. Proposed company windows below are operational pilot conventions, not observed buyer cadence, forecasts or performance targets.

## Proposed company AAARRR measures

Company access and authorization would be required. All values are **unavailable**, not zero. Compare like cohorts only; report counts, exclusions, missing linkage and capture coverage alongside any rate. These descriptive measures cannot establish causal lift by themselves.

| Stage / operational question | Proposed numerator / denominator | Window, grain and required truth |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness: are relevant pages being found? | Query/page impressions and clicks separately; optional clicks ÷ impressions only within the same reporting slice. | Company search-report export, fixed reporting period, page/query/device/geography. Aggregate reporting rows, not identified people. Public scoped queries are a separate monitoring stream. No impressions from our Reddit posts assumed. |
| Acquisition: does a route lead to evaluation? | Distinct eligible landing sessions with an evaluation CTA click ÷ all eligible captured landing sessions. Also report subsequent linked new-account counts separately. | Same consented session, declared 30-minute inactivity session rule. Eligibility tagged independently of click/outcome; broad audiences, bots and staff separate. This click rate is not signup conversion. |
| Activation: does a new account get a useful result? | New eligible analysis accounts with a delivered result explicitly judged usable against their task criteria ÷ new eligible accounts with a mature seven-day observation window. | Start at account creation; seven calendar days is a provisional pilot window. Same account/project and genuine export/share delivery. Delivery alone, generation success or assistant judgment cannot prove usefulness. |
| Retention: is value repeated when another job arises? | Activated accounts with a second distinct project and useful delivered result ÷ activated accounts with a mature 30-day window. | Start at the first accepted usefulness confirmation linked to a delivered result; 30 calendar days is provisional. Retries/duplicate exports of one project are not repeats. Report task cadence/known new-task opportunities separately; no repeat within an arbitrary window is not established churn. |
| Revenue: do eligible unpaid accounts buy paid access? | New eligible analysis accounts without a prior settled paid-access purchase at creation, with a first non-test settled positive plan-access payment in 30 days ÷ those eligible unpaid-at-entry accounts with a mature 30-day window. Report retained paid-access accounts/net receipts separately after refunds. | Billing is authoritative; currency and refund cutoff explicit. Start at account creation. Payment may precede useful output; never require activation to enter this denominator. Pre-entry paid accounts are a separate cohort, not conversion failures; retain unmatched checkout payments separately. This is not LTV, margin or profit. |
| Referral: do introductions lead to value? | New eligible referred accounts reaching useful first delivery in seven days ÷ eligible referred accounts with mature seven-day windows and valid attribution. Report successful referrers separately. | Start at referred account creation; self-referrals, pre-existing accounts, bots/staff and duplicate claims excluded with reasons. Invitations or public shares alone are not successful referrals. Attribution needs a valid consented token/linkage. |

Seven-/30-day windows and the session rule are explicit design proposals. Company review must approve or revise them using actual task cadence, access conditions and data semantics **before cohort collection/comparison**. Version changes create separate cohorts; do not select windows retrospectively for better results. AAARRR is a set of questions, not a guaranteed chronological user path. The earlier funnel map's activated-to-paid concept is a possible secondary diagnostic, not the primary revenue denominator here.

Eligibility proposals, also requiring company approval: landing-session eligibility means an approved evaluation route/version with bots and internal traffic identified separately; it is route eligibility, not a verified qualified buyer. Account eligibility means genuine non-test/non-staff account, confirmed access eligibility and self-reported target product-demo/launch job. Target role/team remains provisional under H04; capture it explicitly if used, never infer it from a page visit. Unknown task/access/identity status stays a separate reported stratum, with coverage counts. Do not select eligible accounts based on completion or payment. Referral eligibility additionally requires a new account and valid attribution, excluding self/pre-existing cases. Broader captured populations may be reported separately, never relabeled as the screened cohort.

For proposed windows, use UTC elapsed days (seven days = 168 hours; 30 days = 720 hours); define entry ≤ outcome ≤ entry plus window. Activation/referral require both delivery and accepted usefulness confirmation within the window. Retention requires a second distinct project created after first useful value, delivered and accepted within the repeat window. Revenue requires payment settlement in its window. Later confirmations/outcomes remain late context, not retroactive in-window successes. A session starts anew after an inactivity gap of at least 30 minutes. Missing source capture before cohort entry makes prior paid status unknown, not unpaid.

Retention entry is the timestamp of the first accepted usefulness confirmation linked to a genuine delivered result, including accounts achieving it after the initial seven days; separate timely/late/unknown time-to-value cohorts. A late activation does not become an in-window activation success, but it is not erased from later usage analysis. For the revenue metric, `payment_kind` distinguishes plan_access, credit_purchase, other and unknown. Only positive settled plan_access qualifies for first paid-access conversion; credit/top-up transactions remain separate cash context, with no assumption these purchase types currently exist. The company must confirm the applicable monetization model before using this classification.

## Proposed event contract and identities

The [event dictionary](measurement-events.csv) is a handoff specification, not event data or installed tracking. Common required properties: unique `event_id`, `event_name`, UTC `occurred_at` and `received_at`, `schema_version`, `source_system`, `environment`, `capture_basis` (app permission or company-approved billing processing), `identity_unit`, and the event's applicable pseudonymous IDs. No raw email, Reddit handle, private customer media, prompts or transcript contents in this repository or analytics payload proposal.

Identity rules:

- `anonymous_id`/`session_id`: consented browser/session identifiers; do not fingerprint or cross-device guess. Unlinked anonymous sessions remain unlinked.
- `user_id`: individual operator. `account_id`: company-approved evaluation/billing analysis unit. Actual user/account/team model is unknown; freeze it before reporting account rates. Record membership as of event time; a shared operator must not become multiple paying customers.
- `project_id`/`output_id`: distinct job and delivered item; confirm their semantics with the company. Do not turn re-renders into new projects to inflate retention.
- `invoice_id`/`refund_id`: billing deduplication and reconciliation, with pseudonymous `billing_customer_id` and `linkage_status`. Authoritative billing records may have null `account_id` before deterministic linkage; preserve them instead of failing capture. Unmatched cash is separate from account conversion. Currency cannot be silently summed across denominations; avoid counting credits/top-ups as new customers.
- `referral_id` plus pseudonymous referring account: company-confirmed permitted linkage, retained without identifying messages. Product referral infrastructure is unobserved, not diagnosed as missing.

Deduplicate on event ID and authoritative delivery/invoice/refund IDs. Use occurrence time for cohorts and received time to flag late events; report snapshot cutoff and restate late refunds/outcomes transparently. Quarantine missing IDs, failed joins, impossible event ordering and unknown eligibility; disclose counts rather than silently dropping inconvenient failures. Technical deduplication is distinct from interpreting one person's repeated feedback.

Property types: IDs/paths/versions/bases are nonidentifying strings; timestamps are ISO-8601 UTC; `amount_minor` is an integer in the currency's declared minor unit and `currency` its code; staff/test flags are boolean or unknown, not silently false. `delivery_kind` is export/share; `usefulness` is accepted/rejected/unclear, recorded by the operator; no response means no usefulness event, not acceptance. `linkage_status` is linked/unmatched/unknown. The usefulness record retains rejection/uncertainty and the criteria version; only accepted outcomes satisfy activation. Additional enums/schema validation need company approval before implementation.

## Attribution, reporting and failure handling

For future company implementation, retain permitted landing-page/version, referrer category and allowlisted campaign tags. Use same-session source for evaluation clicks. For linked signup, propose a last observed non-direct touch within 30 days only where consent and deterministic linkage exist; report direct, unknown/unlinked and overwritten/unavailable origins separately. Source tags describe association, not incremental influence. An unattributed visit is not automatically ChatGPT traffic. Do not attach sensitive search queries to person-level events. AI retrieval checks retain exact prompt/date/model/effort/search state and separate cohorts under [collection method](../research/collection-method.md).

Every company report declares cohort-entry rule, identity grain, eligibility/exclusions fixed independently of success, window/version, mature denominator, outcome count, pending/right-censored cases, missing coverage, timezone/cutoff and source. An account is mature only if entry plus its window is no later than cutoff and required data are captured. Immature cases stay pending, not failures. A zero denominator yields unavailable, not a zero rate. Opted-out or untracked cases cannot be represented as known successes/failures; describe instrumented coverage limitations.

Guardrails: failed/cancelled deliveries; useful-result rejection and actual manual corrections; price/eligibility misunderstanding; task mismatch; refunds/duplicate payments; consent withdrawals; unqualified traffic. Signup or delivery increases without usable results do not establish value. Resolve billing discrepancies against the authoritative ledger; separate settled, failed, pending, refunded and subscription-state records. Do not derive churn/LTV/CAC/ROI without appropriate periods, costs and company data.
