## O01 — Resolve inconsistent public offer information

**Verified observation:** [E028–E031, October 4 refresh](../research/offer-refresh-2026-10-04.md) confirm different article price claims versus the pricing display, plus differing article trial durations. Correct current web-trial terms remain unknown. W01 is the corresponding [hygiene issue](../research/website-hygiene.md). AI10 showed mixed offer retrieval, but its cause is unknown.

**Hypothesis / mechanism (H01):** an authoritative, platform-specific explanation could reduce conflicting information during evaluation. Factual consistency matters even if nobody was confused. A clearer offer does not create a reason to leave a satisfactory incumbent.

**Proposal:** a company owner confirms introductory/ongoing price, billing period, usage limits, platform and trial eligibility; update the three named articles and their CTAs; link volatile terms to pricing. Do not replace unknown trial terms with an invented offer. The local before/after block is an editorial sample, not a deployed conversion page.

**Counterevidence / unknowns:** current pricing already states the ladder; no qualified buyer has attributed abandonment to these inconsistencies. We do not know article traffic, exposure, current checkout terms or payment availability. Mac-access statements cannot be generalized to the web. This is not evidence that the paywall itself should change.

**Conditional validation:** after H03 learning indicates an offer question matters, obtain authorized/consented eligible readers and compare current versus proposed information. Task: explain first versus ongoing monthly price, distinguish editing/generation limits, identify which access terms remain unknown and choose a supported next action. Freeze materials/eligibility/rubric/order before collection; counterbalance exposure order if paired, retain familiarity and learning effects. Primary observation: correct answers and unresolved questions per reader, with exact denominators. Repeated confusion resolved by revised wording warrants refining/retesting that wording; equally clear current material lowers the comprehension priority. No statistical, signup or willingness-to-pay claim. This is a test outline, not an authorized or run EX02.

**Company verification / guardrail:** named-page factual check and owner-confirmed billing/access terms; if later testing commercially, use eligible landing-session-to-next-action counts by source/cohort and billing-confirmed outcomes, with refunds/misleading-expectation guardrails. No live baseline available.

## O02 — Define a proof package buyers can assess

**Observation:** E011/B001 contain a raw-input request, self-reported organizational use and a founder response saying samples/prompts existed. E001 includes public examples; E017 redirects a historical sample route to the homepage. E012–E014/B002–B004 discuss real-UI fidelity and corrections, sometimes alongside promotion or satisfactory manual editing. [Classification and counterevidence](../research/buyer-evidence-review.md). None is our product test.

**Hypothesis / mechanism (H02, gated by H03):** showing provenance and correction steps for an actual relevant job may help a buyer judge fit. It could also reveal that Cardboard is unsuitable; truthful disqualification is useful evaluation, not a failed sale to conceal.

**Proposal:** first define the evaluation package: genuine input recording, exact editing request, genuine output, corrections made, remaining limitations, acceptance criteria and eligible access. Inventory company-owned samples before asking for new material. Reuse existing proof when it answers the job. Independently, prepare a clearly labeled storyboard/specification and ask readers which evidence is missing; do not render simulated Cardboard output.

**Counterevidence / unknowns:** samples may already satisfy the buyer; current in-app sample availability, correction burden, quality and comparative performance are inaccessible. Editing may be unnecessary for a simple demo. A storyboard preference cannot establish performance.

**Validation and directional rule:** EX01 first records actual recent-task acceptance needs and unresolved burdens. If its preregistered adequate-incumbent rule applies, defer broad switching/proof claims. If it identifies a repeated unresolved job, prepare a bounded proof-evaluation protocol before collecting new feedback. Ask qualified readers to distinguish shown evidence from unproven capability and name the remaining objection; record answers/missing information as communication evidence. Genuine output quality or time comparisons require company execution, the same input/task and observed corrections. No completed product test claimed.

**Company verification / guardrail:** confirm samples exist, are lawful to share and reflect current product/plan; observe user-confirmed useful output against task criteria. Report failed outputs/manual work and do not use raw exports as activation. Later retention depends on actual job cadence, not a guessed return window.
