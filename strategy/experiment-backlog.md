## EX02 — Conversion-page comprehension

Hypothesis: the task-specific page may help readers distinguish documented capabilities, illustrative requirements and actual proof. Material: frozen [page v1](../content/conversion/saas-demo-evaluation-page-v1.md), a separate response sheet and a rubric set before collection. Screen for personally making/editing a recent SaaS product video and involvement in workflow choice; record task date, role and eligibility gaps rather than infer them.

A future pilot can reserve five eligible cases in screening order; this is a learning target, not a representative sample. Show only this fixed version initially. Ask one short task at a time: identify the page's job; distinguish the example from product results; select a next step supported by their current needs. Count correct, incorrect, unclear and unanswered responses per task; report attempts and missing cases separately.

Proposed rule: the same material misunderstanding in two distinct eligible cases triggers a revision. Correct answers in at least four of five complete cases permit a small retest of the wording, not a conversion claim. Fewer than five complete cases: case-level learning only. Record if the current editor is sufficient; choosing not to evaluate is not task failure. Stop if the page is read as proven performance or a purchase recommendation. No request for private footage and no account signup.

## EX03 — Offer comprehension

Conditional on a real access/terms question. A company owner first confirms platform, billing, introductory versus ongoing amounts, usage/export rules and applicable free access. Compare the retained dated offer block with a newly verified explanation only after correcting obsolete material; historical copy is not a current commercial offer.

Future pilot: five independently eligible readers with a relevant evaluation task. Counterbalance material order and retain familiarity/exposure. Ask them to explain ongoing versus introductory price, editing versus generation limits and which access term remains unknown. Report each paired answer and missing response, not a pooled percentage improvement.

Proposed rule: repeated same-item confusion in two readers warrants rewriting that item; equally clear current wording lowers priority. Any inaccurate offer statement blocks release. Comprehension cannot establish willingness to pay or lost signup recovery. Materials, owner confirmation and a frozen rubric are prerequisites; nothing is scheduled.

## EX04 — Real correction and handoff comparison

Trigger: a recent completed-project account establishes a specific consequential burden that an adequate incumbent does not resolve. Compare incumbent and candidate on the same permitted recording, request, acceptance criteria and output destination. Preserve version/plan, setup, assets, prompts, correction steps, remaining manual work and failures. Counterbalance order where practical and disclose familiarity.

Primary result is task acceptance after the actual correction, not time to first cut. Time is a secondary observed quantity with explicit start/end boundaries; include setup, inspection, manual fixes, export and review. One operator/project cannot establish average savings. If handoff matters, reopen in the receiving tool and perform the required change.

Proposed rule: any failed mandatory accuracy/control/handoff requirement prevents recommending that workflow for this task. A passing single case warrants replication on another relevant job, not a market or product-fit conclusion. Stop on missing media rights, unsupported access or inconsistent inputs. Requires legitimate product access and a new explicit authorization; denied inputs are not requested in this project.

## EX05 — Article usefulness

Keep articles task-specific: workflow selection, export QA, handoff inventory, documentation comparison and adaptation storyboard. Before company publishing, verify exact-query intent and current canonical coverage; merge rather than duplicate if the same task is already served.

Future pilot: five relevant readers per article, each attempting that article's next action on their own non-sensitive project description. Freeze article/version and scoring before exposure. Record whether the task and next step were understood, unusable fields, unsupported interpretations and missing responses. An optional before/after within-reader task must retain order/learning effects.

Proposed rule: two independent same-task misunderstandings trigger revision; a practical next action completed by four of five readers warrants reuse/retest, not organic traffic or citation predictions. Stop or merge when the article's task is not distinct. Reader usefulness and public discovery are different measurements.

## EX06 — Company route experiment

Only after authorized release and instrumentation. Freeze the eligible route/session definition, source mix, assignment, reporting window, bot/internal exclusions, primary CTA and sample/stop decision before launch. The existing [measurement plan](measurement-plan.md) and [12 event contracts](measurement-events.csv) govern units and deduplication. No arbitrary sample-size guarantee is supplied without a baseline and acceptable effect/error assumptions.

Primary measure: sessions with the specified evaluation action / eligible sessions exposed to the assigned page version. A random split can support a comparison if assignment and capture are valid; a sequential before/after does not establish causality without confound control. Report absolute counts, uncertainty, exposure imbalance and capture gaps; do not declare a winner when evidence is insufficient.

Guardrails: misunderstood access/proof, unsuitable switching, inaccurate claims, broken destinations and loss of required editing control. Activation requires user-confirmed useful delivery, retention a second distinct job, revenue authoritative settlement and referral a linked new account reaching value. CTA clicks and exports alone do not replace those outcomes. No company events or rates exist in this work sample.
