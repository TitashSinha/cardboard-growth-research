2026-10-03; D008–D009; H03–H04 in [assumptions](../assumptions.md). No participant validation.

|---|---|---|---|

| Small agencies | Vendor collaboration/handoff offers and Q12/AI05 indicate a plausible job | Client permissions, approvals, reusable branding and project delivery may dominate first-cut speed; Cardboard controls not tested | Defer as first cohort: weak direct behavior evidence and greater handoff dependencies. | Professional creators | Public product category and AI06 indicate a plausible long-recording/editing job | Familiar suites, Descript/Opus specialization, style consistency and long-project reliability | Defer as first cohort: output/style and economics cannot be tested here, and this evidence set has little comparable firsthand task detail. |

The selection is for research, not a commercial targeting claim. The provisional task is the last SaaS feature/launch video personally produced or edited within 90 days. Trigger: an actual release/deadline or UI update. Alternative: the tools, teammate, freelancer or simple recording actually used; record rather than assign an incumbent in advance.

Screen for personal production/editing involvement, concrete completed project, influence on tool/workflow choice and a small-team context. For this study use self-reported 1–10 people doing marketing/content as an operational screening definition, not a market fact. Founders doing the task may qualify; record their role separately. Record available desktop/browser, Cardboard familiarity, competing-tool affiliation and project date. A commissioning-only account can inform a separate context interview but is not pooled with the main hands-on cohort.

Provisional positioning question: **Which recent product-video task, if any, remains difficult enough to justify changing the workflow?** Do not yet claim that Cardboard saves time, preserves UI perfectly or offers exclusive chat/timeline features.

Next-action logic: keep an adequate incumbent; investigate a specific unresolved editing/correction task; or clarify an actual access/offer objection. The selected workflow test must decide which branch deserves the next asset. Proposed primary channel is task-specific search/content, supported by transparent product demonstrations; neither is validated or live. Community contact is research preparation only and requires authorization.

What changes this choice: eligible buyers have no repeated material problem, role screening contradicts the cohort, or concrete agency/creator evidence proves a stronger bounded task. See the preregistered [test](../experiments/workflow-fit/protocol.md).
