## How the work was divided

Titash set the purpose and constraints, chose priorities, challenged assumptions, directed communication and decided the delivery format. He used Codex to investigate, propose approaches and execute the work. He also reported manual distribution/outreach and supplied four external article drafts.

Codex researched sources, prepared analysis and options, operated authorized browser actions, drafted and revised materials, designed prospective tests/measurement and built and checked the deliverables. Titash's project ownership includes recorded selection and authorization of AI-proposed approaches; it does not mean every idea, methodology or implementation detail originated with him.

## What this demonstrates

The project demonstrates Titash's scope-setting, prioritization, critical judgment, audience/copy direction and management of AI-assisted work. Codex's research, synthesis, drafting and implementation remain disclosed. Independent personal execution of the final synthesis, copy or experimental design is not established merely by directing or approving it.

Actual replies were collected, analyzed and applied to content decisions. The original structured EX01 test was not performed. No hands-on Cardboard product test, company instrumentation or measured acquisition/conversion/revenue result is claimed. Proposed features and pricing remain hypotheses.

Outreach is closed. Identities, exact messages, private mappings and screenshots stay outside Git. Article review and any later Docs-to-repository reconciliation remain separate work. A personally completed decision memo or documented copy revision is a proposed next demonstration, not a completed contribution.
