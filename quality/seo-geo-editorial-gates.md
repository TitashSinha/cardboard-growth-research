# SEO and GEO editorial gates for the five articles

Prepared October 4, 2026; D020. Standard for drafts and a later authorized company handoff, not five finished articles or a promise to outrank competitors. [Primary guidance](../research/seo-geo-guidance-2026-10-04.md), [coverage](../research/coverage-matrix.md) and [measurement](../strategy/measurement-plan.md) are the supporting records. Buyer feedback remains upstream of four further briefs.

## Choose the opportunity before adding keywords

1. Define the buyer, actual task, trigger, incumbent and useful next action. Keep H03's satisfactory-workflow alternative. Traffic without a relevant job is not our goal.
2. Give each article one primary query hypothesis and a small set of related questions/entities. Explain whether it needs a guide, checklist or comparison. Query wording alone does not prove demand; no invented volume/difficulty score.
3. Check intent against retained search observations. Before company publication, inspect current results for the chosen exact query under recorded settings and retain actual URLs/types/sections promptly. A web search is not a live Google rank test. Do not reconstruct lost field notes or extend research merely to reach a quota.
4. Compare the useful answers, missing evidence and next actions of competing pages; choose a demonstrable contribution. Retain specific URLs and dates before claiming our content is better. A narrower, more useful answer is a competition hypothesis, not a guaranteed ranking opportunity.
5. Assign one intended canonical page per intent. Compare against existing Cardboard coverage and the other four slots; merge/link where the same need is already answered. Multiple distinct wordings do not automatically justify multiple pages.

## Draft and revision acceptance

| Gate | Required inspectable evidence | Failure / action |
|---|---|---|
| Keyword and intent | Primary/supporting query hypotheses, buyer job, page type, demand/competition evidence status and overlap treatment in the brief | Do not call a question researched when it is only a proposed wording. Reframe if the page cannot fulfill the query promise. |
| Useful answer | Direct, accurate opening; sections answering the task; an applicable decision/checklist/example and appropriate next step | An answer-first paragraph alone is insufficient. Add useful analysis without fabricating outputs, quotes or results. |
| Natural language | Descriptive title/H1, proposed slug/meta description and purposeful headings; topic terms where they help readers | No keyword-density target, repeated exact-match stuffing or rigid word count. Metadata is a draft proposal, not deployed HTML or controlled snippets. |
| Evidence and authorship | Claim-to-source trail with dates/limits; vendor claims versus public self-reports versus proposed analysis; disclosed AI assistance | No hands-on reviewer credentials or buyer-research findings without the corresponding work. Don't change dates merely to look fresh. |
| AI-search readability | Clear entities, self-contained explanations, labeled observations/unknowns, descriptive source links; table/list when genuinely useful | No guaranteed citation score, fixed passage length or claim that a formatting trick causes retrieval. Questions/answers serve readers, not hidden keyword blocks. |
| Proof and correction burden | Genuine examples only, or clearly illustrative checklist/storyboard; alternative strengths and limits retained | No simulated Cardboard output or product advantage. No study/percentage drawn from an unsourced skill statistic. |
| Links and conversion | Relevant existing routes and a concrete, truthful next action; unresolved access terms explicit | Repo-relative worksheet links need a public destination before company publishing. No universal free-trial CTA or automatic switching pitch. |
| Human/buyer iteration | Original retained; each internal edit labeled; actual feedback and later change reasoning recorded separately | Internal SEO editing is not the required buyer-driven revision. Feedback can change keyword/segment/format decisions. |

## Company publication checks — outside current authorization

The company would need to confirm actual public URL/canonical, index/snippet eligibility, robots and hosting/CDN behavior, accessible rendered main text, internal links, mobile usability, suitable media and markup that matches visible content. Reuse the [hygiene audit](../research/website-hygiene.md): robots/sitemap/llms.txt already exist in the retained sample; do not propose adding existing files or imply they guarantee inclusion. OAI-SearchBot search access is distinct from GPTBot training. No special AI files or schema are prerequisites under the checked Google guidance.

An authorized company owner would also approve proof, current price/access claims, author attribution and CTA destinations. No site edits, publication, new accounts, paid SEO tools, fabricated reviews, link purchases or manufactured community mentions are part of this work.

Competing beyond the draft also needs a credible public publishing context and genuine distribution: documented expertise, useful supporting pages and earned references where the material merits them. These are proposed company responsibilities, not an authority score we have measured. Our community questions collect language/alternatives; they are not a backlink or branded-mention campaign. No editorial checklist can promise superiority over every other page, and private repository files do not have public search visibility.

## Measure and revise after genuine execution

Before publication, retain the chosen query/prompt cohort and source/competitor baseline. For new observations save exact query/prompt, date, engine/model/effort/search state, requested/observed geography and actual result/source details. Keep unlike cohorts separate. The existing Google notes lack full result lists and observed geography; they cannot be upgraded retrospectively.

After authorized company publication, measure query/page impressions and clicks, eligible route sessions/evaluation actions, and relevant downstream value using the measurement plan. For AI search, retain whether Cardboard is mentioned/recommended and whether the article's exact URL is cited, separately. Do not treat sources for the whole answer as Cardboard citations. ChatGPT source tags/referrals, where observable, are attribution signals; missing attribution is unknown. Google AI features are included in its overall Web Search Console reporting in the inspected guidance, so do not promise a separately isolated AI lift from that report.

Company review should freeze a reporting period and comparison design before results, using actual indexing/traffic availability; no arbitrary rank-by-date guarantee. A ranking move without relevant visits/actions does not prove growth, and an unpublished revision cannot cause a measured visibility improvement. Wrong intent, no distinct value or poor next-action comprehension triggers revision/merge, not more keyword repetitions. Distinguish observation from causal impact.
