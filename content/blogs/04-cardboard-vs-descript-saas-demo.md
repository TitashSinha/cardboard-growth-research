# Cardboard vs Descript for a narrated SaaS demo: what to verify

Both products' documentation describes AI assistance and manual editing, but through different editing and handoff mechanisms. Documentation cannot tell you which performs better on your footage, so compare one correction task and the output you need.

This article uses published documentation only. It is not a purchase recommendation, and keeping your current editor is a valid result. For the earlier question of whether to evaluate anything, see [How to choose a video editor for SaaS product demos](../revisions/01-workflow-switch-evidence-v1.2.md).

## Define one shared task

**Illustrative evaluation setup (not executed):** take a real screen recording of your product with narration. Add one terminology correction, such as a renamed feature that changes both narration and captions, and one scene-timing change, such as holding a screen longer. Decide the output you need: a shareable link, an MP4 or a timeline file for another editor. Use the same footage and the same requests for both products.

## Compare the documented starting and editing mechanisms

**Cardboard.** Its [homepage](https://www.cardboard.ai/) describes building an editable first cut from a prompt, footage or both, then refining it through chat requests or on the timeline. Its [Mac page](https://www.cardboard.ai/desktop) describes seeing the plan before an edit runs, undoing changes and taking over on the timeline.

**Descript.** Its [pricing page](https://www.descript.com/pricing) lists text-based editing, an AI co-editor called Underlord and video generation on listed plans. Its [Scene Editor documentation](https://help.descript.com/visuals/scene-editor-overview) describes selecting visual elements to move, resize or restyle.

So neither is simply "AI" versus "manual." Both describe assistance and hands-on control; the question is how each handles your correction.

## Compare correction and handoff questions

| Requirement | Cardboard documentation | Descript documentation | Still to verify |
|---|---|---|---|
| Change narration wording | Chat or timeline refinement described; voiceover listed in the plan comparison | Text-based editing described | Whether narration, captions and timing update together on your footage |
| Change one scene's crop | Timeline editing described; element-level controls not detailed in pages checked | Layer toolbar for size, position and crop; media replacement | Whether labels stay readable in the export |
| Change one scene's timing | Chat/timeline refinement described; timing-specific behavior unverified | Not established by the Scene Editor source cited here | Whether the timing change preserves the intended narration and sequence |
| Caption delivery | Captions listed on every plan | SRT/VTT subtitle files documented separately from captions that stay on the video | Which form your destination needs |
| Move the project to another editor | "NLE exports" listed from Starter; formats and transferred elements not stated in pages checked | Timeline export to several editors on Creator and above; chart lists animation, transitions, images and scenes as unsupported for three video editors | Your exact target format; reopen it and make a correction |
| Team review | Sharing by link described | Commenting and live collaboration listed on plans | Reviewer access and permissions |

A blank or vague documentation cell is not proof a feature is missing. Treat it as a question to verify.

Correcting transcript text is different from changing the spoken narration. For a terminology change, verify the replacement-audio route and whether captions and timing stay aligned.

## Check platform, plan, export and agent requirements

- **Platform.** Cardboard's homepage FAQ describes any computer with Chrome plus a Mac app for Apple Silicon, with phones and tablets not yet supported. Confirm Descript's requirements for your devices.
- **Plan limits.** Cardboard's pricing page shows Lite with three exports at 1080p and generative AI not included. Descript's documentation limits timeline export to Creator, Business and Enterprise plans, and its pricing page does not list watermark-free export on Free.
- **Connected agents.** Cardboard's Mac page describes connecting Claude or Codex and says an eligible paid plan is required.
- **Free options.** Cardboard says Lite is free with the Mac download and needs no card. Descript's pricing page says its Free plan needs no card. These are free tiers with their own limits, not shown to be trials of paid features.
- **Prices.** Skip price numbers until you know the billing basis. Cardboard shows launch pricing for the first month, then a monthly rate. Descript shows monthly and annual views. Compare the same basis and the plan you would actually need.

## Run a small, fair comparison

When you have legitimate access, run the shared task in both products with the same source recording and correction requests. Record each step, the manual work that remained, and what the output looked like when reopened in its target. Include failures. Keep your current editor as the baseline.

This comparison has not been run. Neither product was tested for this article.
