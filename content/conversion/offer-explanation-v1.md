## Editorial rationale and release gates

E028 supplies the table and plan distinctions. The change separates introductory/ongoing price, links to one pricing authority, and removes an unverified universal trial promise. For reusable blog copy, prefer a short link to pricing instead of duplicating the whole ladder; the explicit table here supports a reviewable sample.

Current correct web-trial terms are unknown. Mac/access facts in E006 are an earlier public snapshot, not refreshed here or generalizable to browser access. Company confirmation is required before adding platform-specific eligibility, free-access or trial copy. Annual billing, taxes, payment availability, checkout and actual use remain untested. A pricing-page link does not promise an available free trial or a successful payment.

This is factual/editorial cleanup, not evidence that readers were confused. It may leave an adequate-workflow buyer correctly choosing no new tool. Full proof, objections and CTA placement depend on buyer evidence and would belong in a later conversion-page sample.
