## Why this page

The original offer block addresses verified factual inconsistency. The page addresses a different question: what evidence would help someone evaluate an editable first-cut workflow for their own SaaS recording? [Exploratory replies](../../experiments/workflow-fit/collection-closeout-2026-10-06.md) inform attention to production stages, control and corrections; they do not establish an eligible-cohort switching need or product advantage.

| Observation / requirement | Copy choice | Remaining uncertainty |
|---|---|---|
| Planning, assets, animation and polish occur as separate jobs in maker accounts | Start with one recording/task; preserve manual-control and adequate-incumbent options | Target-cohort prevalence and consequences unknown |
| Generated assets and editable finishing are distinct in some accounts | Inspect source, output, corrections and handoff separately | Cardboard's actual performance not tested |
| Source-linked examples already exist | Ask whether existing proof answers the task rather than claim no examples | Suitability/accessibility of full proof packages unverified |
| Volatile offer terms and different access routes | Link to current official terms rather than repeat a price ladder or universal trial | Actual entitlement, checkout and transfer fidelity unknown |

The main CTA navigates to an illustrative section that exists in the sample. Final links resolve to official information pages. No CTA offers an invented demonstration, starts an account, recommends purchase or implies measured growth.
